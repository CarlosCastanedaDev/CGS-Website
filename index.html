Who We Are

A Commitment to Quality Living in South Berwyn

CGS Properties was founded on a simple belief: every resident deserves a home that is clean, comfortable, and well-maintained — without compromise. For over a decade, we have been proud stewards of residential properties in the South Berwyn community.

Our mission is to provide exceptional, affordable housing that our residents are genuinely proud to call home. We maintain our properties to a high standard, respond to maintenance needs promptly, and treat every tenant with the respect and attentiveness they deserve.

Rooted in the South Berwyn neighborhood, we are not a distant management company — we are your neighbors. We believe that great housing is the foundation of a great community, and we take that responsibility seriously.
